Privacy Policy — FAR AIM Pocket

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application FAR AIM Pocket (package name com.faraimpocket.app, "the app") published by Amin Loungou. It describes, in plain language, what information the app handles — and, just as importantly, what it does not.

1. The short version

  • The app does not collect, use, transmit, or share any personal or device data.
  • The app works entirely offline and contains no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics.
  • Everything you create inside the app (bookmarks, notes, highlights) stays on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • We cannot see your data and have no technical access to it.

2. Information the app stores — on your device only

The app reads a bundled, offline copy of United States aviation regulations (Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations and Title 49 Part 830) and stores the following information exclusively in the app's private storage area on your device:

  • Bookmarks — the regulation sections you save;
  • Notes — text you write about a section;
  • Highlights — text selections you mark;
  • App preferences — your chosen regulation edition and appearance settings (for example, light or dark mode or your pinned edition);
  • A local search index built from the bundled regulations, to make offline search work.

This information is local application data, not personal data sent to a server. The app does not run when a network connection is absent, and it never sends your content anywhere. Your operating system also protects this storage: other apps cannot read it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or erase its data.

3. Information the app does not collect

The app does not use, embed, or call any of the following:

  • third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or marketing SDKs;
  • advertising networks or identifiers;
  • location services, camera, microphone, contacts, or any other device sensors or personal data sources;
  • user accounts, logins, or registration flows;
  • cookies or cross-site tracking technology.

Consequently, no data processed by the app — including any personal data — is collected by us, and none is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party.

4. Required access

Because the app is fully offline and self-contained, it requests no Android permissions. It does not access the Internet, your storage, or any other resource outside its own sandbox.

5. Content sources and independence

The regulatory text bundled in the app is public-domain material of the United States government. FAR AIM Pocket is an independent study and reference tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official source of the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) or any other government agency. Always verify current requirements against official FAA publications before flying or acting on them.

6. Children

The app is a professional training reference intended for adults. It does not direct advertising, marketing, or any interactive collection feature at children under 13, and because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly gather information from anyone, child or adult.

7. In-app purchases (planned feature)

A future update may add optional purchase of advanced study tools. If and when that ships, you will be clearly told before it goes live in that update; when it does, this policy will be updated to reflect it. Even then:

  • payments will be processed exclusively by Google Play Billing under Google's own privacy policy — we will never see or store your payment details;
  • your purchase entitlement will be held locally on your device;
  • the regulations themselves (reading and search) will always remain free — purchases will never be required to access regulatory text.

8. Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device only. To remove it completely, either uninstall the app or use your device's Settings → Apps → FAR AIM Pocket → Storage → Clear data. There is no server copy for us to retain, hand over, or delete on request, because none exists.

9. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU) and the CCPA (California) grant rights to access, correct, erase, or port personal data. Because the app never collects personal data, there is no data held by us to exercise those rights against. Should we ever begin collecting data (for example, with the planned purchases), this policy will be updated first and the relevant rights and contact points will be described here.

10. Changes to this policy

The latest revision of this policy is always on this page under the same URL, and material updates will also be announced in the app's release notes. The "effective date" above reflects the current version.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy or this app? Write to aminloungou@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few business days.
