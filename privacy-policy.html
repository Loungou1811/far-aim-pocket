Privacy Policy — FAR AIM Pocket

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application FAR AIM Pocket (package name com.faraimpocket.app, "the app") published by Amin Loungou. It describes, in plain language, what information the app handles — and, just as importantly, what it does not.

1. The short version

  • Today, the app does not collect, use, transmit, or share any personal or device data.
  • It works entirely offline and contains no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics.
  • Everything you create inside the app (bookmarks, notes, highlights) stays on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • We cannot see your data and have no technical access to it.
  • In a future update we will add optional analytics and crash-reporting built on Google Firebase. We disclose every service and data type involved — in full detail — in §5 below, before we ship a single line of it. When that update lands, the "does not collect" statement in this section is replaced by a pointer to §5, and Google's services start applying as described there.

2. Information the app stores — on your device only

The app reads a bundled, offline copy of United States aviation regulations (Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations and Title 49 Part 830) and stores the following information exclusively in the app's private storage area on your device:

  • Bookmarks — the regulation sections you save;
  • Notes — text you write about a section;
  • Highlights — text selections you mark;
  • App preferences — your chosen regulation edition and appearance settings (for example, light or dark mode or your pinned edition);
  • A local search index built from the bundled regulations, to make offline search work.

This information is local application data, not personal data sent to a server. The app does not run when a network connection is absent, and it never sends your content anywhere. Your operating system also protects this storage: other apps cannot read it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or erase its data.

3. Information the app does not collect

The app does not use, embed, or call any of the following (all statements in this section describe the app as it works today; §5 explains what changes when the planned Firebase services ship):

  • third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or marketing SDKs;
  • advertising networks or identifiers;
  • location services, camera, microphone, contacts, or any other device sensors or personal data sources;
  • user accounts, logins, or registration flows;
  • cookies or cross-site tracking technology.

Consequently, no data processed by the app — including any personal data — is collected by us, and none is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party.

4. Required access

Because the app is fully offline and self-contained, it requests no Android permissions. It does not access the Internet, your storage, or any other resource outside its own sandbox.

When the planned Firebase services (§5) ship, the app will start using an Internet connection for exactly those features — analytics events, crash reports, messages, and any cloud sync we announce on the listing — and that permission change will appear in the store listing at the same update, with this policy already covering it.

5. Google Firebase — planned analytics and stability services (disclosed in advance)

A future update of the app will integrate services from Google Firebase. This section discloses every planned product, the data involved, its purpose, and your controls, so that this policy remains valid on the day integration ships and you are never surprised. None of the processing described here happens until that update; today, §1–§4 above remain an accurate description of the app.

5.1 Products planned and their data

Firebase Crashlytics Crash stack traces; device model, OS version, app version; recent in-app "breadcrumb" events; the same per-install identifier Detect and fix crashes — a regulation reader must never fail on a checkride morning

Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore / Cloud Storage (optional, only if a cloud-sync or account sign-in feature is announced) Account identifier you create (e.g. a Google ID); the notes/bookmarks synchronised in the cloud; timestamps Optional cloud backup of your notes/highlights across devices — never a requirement to use the app offline

5.2 Data controller role

Google, as the Firebase processor, handles the data described above under Google's Firebase Terms, the Google Cloud/DPA, and Google Firebase's product-privacy documentation. We process it only to operate and improve the app; we do not combine it with anything personal, because we hold no personal data; we do not sell it; and we do not use it for advertising. If a future feature required advertising identifiers, this policy would be amended first.

5.3 Retention

Analytics event data is kept no longer than Google's configurable retention for Firebase (typically 2 months, maximum 14); crash logs and their metadata are kept in Crashlytics for up to 90 days; FCM tokens live only while the app install is alive and are invalidated on uninstall or manual data reset.

5.4 Your controls — shipped together with the SDK

  • An in-app "Share usage analytics" toggle and a separate "Share crash reports" toggle, both reachable from Settings. When off, Firebase collection can no longer take place for that device (Analytics collection is disabled at the SDK level prior to any event upload).
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing app data, resets the per-install identifier and deletes locally stored Firebase data.
  • The app is never required by Firebase features: with all toggles off the regulations remain fully readable and searchable on the device.

5.5 Independence unchanged

None of the Firebase processing affects the regulatory contents. Our commitment stands: no advertising SDK, no data sale, no cross-app tracking, no profile building against your reading habits — analytics exist to make the reference better, never to monetise you.

6. Content sources and independence

The regulatory text bundled in the app is public-domain material of the United States government. FAR AIM Pocket is an independent study and reference tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official source of the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) or any other government agency. Always verify current requirements against official FAA publications before flying or acting on them.

7. Children

The app is a professional training reference intended for adults. It does not direct advertising, marketing, or any interactive collection feature at children under 13, and because it collects no data at all, it does not knowingly gather information from anyone, child or adult.

8. In-app purchases (planned feature)

A future update may add optional purchase of advanced study tools. If and when that ships, you will be clearly told before it goes live in that update; when it does, this policy will be updated to reflect it. Even then:

  • payments will be processed exclusively by Google Play Billing under Google's own privacy policy — we will never see or store your payment details;
  • your purchase entitlement will be held locally on your device;
  • the regulations themselves (reading and search) will always remain free — purchases will never be required to access regulatory text.

9. Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device only. To remove it completely, either uninstall the app or use your device's Settings → Apps → FAR AIM Pocket → Storage → Clear data. There is no server copy for us to retain, hand over, or delete on request, because none exists.

Once the planned Firebase services (§5) are active, the corresponding Google-held data is also removed by the same actions — uninstalling or clearing app data resets the per-install Firebase identifiers and stops their collection.

10. Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR (EU) and the CCPA (California) grant rights to access, correct, erase, or port personal data. Because the app never collects personal data, there is no data held by us to exercise those rights against. Should we ever begin collecting data (for example, with the planned purchases or the Firebase services described in §5), this policy will be updated first and the relevant rights and contact points will be described here.

11. Changes to this policy

The latest revision of this policy is always on this page under the same URL, and material updates will also be announced in the app's release notes. The "effective date" above reflects the current version.
