Privacy Policy — FAR AIM Pocket

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application FAR AIM Pocket (package name com.faraimpocket.app, "the app") published by Amin Loungou. It describes what information the app handles and the controls available to you.

1. The short version

  • The regulation reader works offline. The app has no accounts or advertising.
  • On Android, optional Google Firebase Analytics is off by default and starts only if you enable “Share usage analytics” in Settings.
  • Android push notifications are off by default and start only if you enable “App update notifications” and grant Android's notification permission.
  • Everything you create inside the app (bookmarks, notes, highlights) stays on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Firebase features require an internet connection. Reading and searching the bundled regulations do not.

2. Information the app stores — on your device only

The app reads a bundled, offline copy of United States aviation regulations (Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations and Title 49 Part 830) and stores the following information exclusively in the app's private storage area on your device:

  • Bookmarks — the regulation sections you save;
  • Notes — text you write about a section;
  • Highlights — text selections you mark;
  • App preferences — your chosen regulation edition and appearance settings (for example, light or dark mode or your pinned edition), plus your local Analytics and notification consent choices;
  • A local search index built from the bundled regulations, to make offline search work.

This information is local application data and is never sent to Firebase. Your operating system protects this storage: other apps cannot read it, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or erase its data.

3. Information the app does not send to Firebase

  • your search text or search results;
  • the exact sections you read (section-level citations) — usage events carry only the regulation part, e.g. Part 91;
  • bookmark, note, or highlight contents;
  • advertising identifiers or data for ad targeting.

The app also does not use location, camera, microphone, or contacts; it has no account or login feature. Firebase Analytics receives only the limited event and technical data described below when you opt in.

4. Required access

The app uses Android's Internet permission for optional Firebase Analytics and push messaging. Internet access is not required to read or search the bundled regulations. Android notification permission is requested only after you turn on app update notifications in Settings.

5. Google Firebase — optional Analytics and Cloud Messaging

Firebase features are available only in builds configured for the developer's Firebase project. Analytics collection and push registration are both off by default. They begin only after the separate opt-ins described below. Firebase Crashlytics, Remote Config, Performance Monitoring, Authentication, and cloud storage are not integrated.

5.1 Products and data

Firebase product Data it processes Purpose
Google Analytics for Firebase A Firebase app-instance identifier; standard app/session events; screen names (route paths only); the regulation part opened (e.g. 14:61 — never the section number); basic event names (search performed, edition changed, bookmark/note/highlight added, notification opened); app/device/OS details and network metadata Firebase processes. Search text, section-level citations, and personal annotations are not included. Understand aggregate feature usage and improve the app.
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) A per-install push registration token and delivery metadata. FCM may process the notification payload needed to deliver an update. Deliver occasional general app/content update notifications through the public_updates topic.

5.2 Controls and service provider

  • Analytics: enable or disable “Share usage analytics” in Settings. When off, the app disables Firebase Analytics collection.
  • Push: enable or disable “App update notifications” in Settings. Enabling it requests Android notification permission. Turning it off unsubscribes from the update topic, disables FCM auto-initialization, and asks Firebase to delete the registration token.
  • First-launch sheet: a one-time in-app sheet invites you to turn either service on (once more a week later if both remain off; never more than twice). Both switches start off, and the sheet changes nothing until you flip one.
  • Google processes the opted-in data to provide Firebase under its applicable terms and privacy documentation. We do not use it for ads, sell it, or attach it to your local reading content.

5.3 Retention

Analytics retention follows the Firebase project settings. Turning off collection stops future Analytics collection; it does not instantly erase events already received by Firebase. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local preferences and the local FCM token, but does not necessarily erase previously received Analytics data.

6. Content sources and independence

The regulatory text bundled in the app is public-domain material of the United States government. FAR AIM Pocket is an independent study and reference tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official source of the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) or any other government agency. Always verify current requirements against official FAA publications before flying or acting on them.

7. Children

The app is a professional training reference intended for adults. It does not target children under 13.

8. In-app purchases (planned feature)

A future update may add optional purchase of advanced study tools. If and when that ships, you will be clearly told before it goes live in that update; when it does, this policy will be updated to reflect it. Even then:

  • payments will be processed exclusively by Google Play Billing under Google's own privacy policy — we will never see or store your payment details;
  • your purchase entitlement will be held locally on your device;
  • the regulations themselves (reading and search) will always remain free — purchases will never be required to access regulatory text.

9. Deleting your data

To remove local bookmarks, notes, highlights, preferences, and the local FCM token, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. You can also turn off Analytics or notifications in the app at any time. Analytics events already received by Firebase are subject to the Firebase project's retention settings. For privacy requests, contact us using the details below.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact us with privacy requests using the details below. Analytics data already held by Firebase is also subject to Google's applicable Firebase terms and retention settings.
